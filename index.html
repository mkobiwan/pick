Look & Find

Say two objects, like “dog or cat”. The first appears on the left, the second on the right, and a counter starts. Look at the one you choose until the ring fills – it moves to the center and bursts into fireworks. (Saying just one object shows it in the center.) The photo whose file name best matches your words is shown; if no photo matches, a built-in picture is used.
